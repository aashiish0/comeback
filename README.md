# Comeback

**Bring your customers back. Automatically.**

Comeback is a lightweight re-engagement tool built for small service businesses — restaurants, salons, barbershops, nail salons, food carts, and more.

---

## What it does

Most small businesses lose customers not because of bad service, but because there's no system to bring them back. Comeback fixes that.

- **Tracks every customer** and scores them as Active, At Risk, or Lost based on how long it's been since their last visit
- **Sends automated SMS messages** when a customer goes quiet — no action needed from the owner
- **Shows recovered customers** and estimated revenue brought back each month
- **Adapts to any service business** with industry presets that configure the right timing thresholds automatically

---

## Features

### Re-engagement engine
The core product. At-risk alerts, automated message queue, live activity feed showing every send and recovery.

### Analytics
- **Customers** — full customer list with Active / At Risk / Lost filters
- **ROI Recap** — auto-generated monthly summary emailed to the owner showing recovered customers and estimated revenue
- **Data** — visit patterns by day and hour, spend heatmap, customer value segments, cohort retention, and smart insights
